Cover performance, inventory lifecycle and attack accuracy

- Cache cover geometry in map coordinates; zoom/pan reprojects the existing overlay, stationary refreshes reuse it, and native closest-collision checks reduce LOS work. Replace per-ray shadow wedges with one clipped shadow polygon per cover wall.
- Targeting lines change colour without labels. Apply only one nearest Soft/Hard Cover wall within 5 ft of the target. Total Cover and native LOS continue to block attacks. Keep existing wall definitions.
- Melee ignores physical cover bonuses, uses token-edge reach and retains defensive dodge; base Attack DC stays 14.
- Range falloff increases ranged DC from +0 at optimal to +6 at maximum. New Range falloff setting can restore the rulebook’s fixed +2 beyond optimal.
- Items can consume a parent quantity when used and remove themselves when exhausted. Empty ammo is removed after successful use, with rollback on chat failure. Consumed-source damage cards retain their damage data.
- Weapons with ammo/charges are separate items. Legacy stacks split into independent copies, quantity increases add separate weapons, and transfer rollback removes all created copies.
- Expanded regressions for zoom/cache reuse, nearest cover, melee, falloff, consumption and independent weapon state. CI runs only as part of release.
